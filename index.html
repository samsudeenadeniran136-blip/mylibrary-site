Questions

Is it safe to install?

Your phone warns about any app that doesn’t come from Google Play. Download only from this page and check the web address first.

Do I need internet?

Building guides, flashcards and tests, and asking questions, need internet. Guides and flashcards you already built stay on your phone.

What if I run out for the week?

Reading and reviewing never run out. For new guides or tests, wait for next week or buy a small pack inside the app.

How do I pay?

Inside the app, with Paystack. Your plan starts once the payment is confirmed.

Can I get a refund?

The refund rules will be on the Refund policy page before launch.

Which phones work?

Android phones only, for now.
