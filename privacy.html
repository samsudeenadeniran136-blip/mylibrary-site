Privacy policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Mylibrary is an app that builds study guides, flashcards and tests from the files you add. This page explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: what you give us when you make your account, such as your name, email or phone number, and the study level you choose.
  • Your content: the files you add, the guides, flashcards and tests built from them, and the questions you ask.
  • Payment status: which plan you bought and when. Payments are handled by Paystack. We do not see or store your card or bank details.
  • Usage and device information: how you use the app, your app version and basic device details, so we can fix problems and keep the service working.

How we use it

  • To build your study material and answer your questions.
  • To run your account, plan and weekly limits.
  • To give support and fix problems.
  • To prevent misuse and keep the service secure.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share only what is needed with services that help us run Mylibrary: AI providers that process your files and questions to create your study material, Paystack for payments, and hosting and storage providers. We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and content while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and content at any time.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you can ask to see, correct or delete your information, object to certain uses, or complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Message us on WhatsApp to make a request.

Your files

Only add files you have the right to use. Do not add files that contain other people’s private information unless you are allowed to.

Children

Mylibrary is made for students. If you are under 18, please use it with the knowledge of a parent or guardian.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
