Refund policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

We want you to get what you paid for. Here is when we refund.

We will refund or fix it when

  • You paid but your plan did not start within 24 hours.
  • You were charged twice for the same plan or pack.
  • A fault on our side stopped you from using your plan, and we could not fix it within 3 days of your report.
  • You changed your mind within 24 hours of paying, and you have not built any guides, flashcards or tests with your plan.

We cannot refund when

  • You have already used your plan’s pages, tests or questions.
  • Your weekly limit ran out. Limits reset every week, and extra packs are available in the app.
  • You bought an extra pack and already used part of it.

How to ask

Message us on WhatsApp within 7 days of the payment. Send the email or phone number on your account, the date, and your Paystack payment reference. We reply within 3 working days.

How you get your money

Approved refunds go back to the way you paid, through Paystack. Your bank may take a few working days to show it.
